    /// Move the `take` best candidates to the front, ranked *within* time
    /// strata rather than across the whole block.
    ///
    /// **Ranking across the whole block instead is a defect**, not a
    /// simplification: the top `take` by amplitude has no reason to be spread
    /// across the block, and loud notes cluster in time -- chords, downbeats --
    /// so the admitted set piles up at the block's opening and leaves its tail
    /// thin. That shipped in 0.2.0 and was audible; see the development notes.
    ///
    /// The strata decide *when*, exactly as `spawn_pick` would; the key decides
    /// *which candidate within each stratum*. Both properties at once rather
    /// than one traded for the other.
    ///
    /// Stratum `s` is `[s*want/strata, (s+1)*want/strata)` with a proportional
    /// quota each. The count trades the two properties off: one stratum per
    /// output slot over-constrains the choice and ranking degenerates, while
    /// too few brings back the clustering. At 64 a 4096-frame block is cut into
    /// 64-frame pieces -- 1.3 ms, far too short to hear as clustering -- and
    /// each picks its own top share. The development notes have the comparison.
