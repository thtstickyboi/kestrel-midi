//! MIDI Tuning Standard scale/octave tuning: a SysEx that moves each of the
//! twelve pitch classes of the channels it names by up to a semitone, the same
//! in every octave.
//!
//! **BASSMIDI reads these, so Kestrel does.** Measured against it on
//! 2026-10-05, on a one-note file: the real-time form
//! (`F0 7F <dev> 08 09 ...`) and its one-byte sibling (`08 08`) both retune the
//! channels in the mask and no others, the non-real-time header (`7E`) does the
//! same, a note already sounding is left where it was, and a GM System On or a
//! GS Reset puts the tuning back. The 2-byte form moved C by -38.77 cents for a
//! message that says -38.71; the 1-byte form by -39.08 for one that rounds to
//! -39 -- within what an FFT of a played note can say.
//!
//! The 31-EDO files that the template writes carry three of these, one for
//! each of the three channels an instrument plays on, so reading them is most
//! of what it takes to play such a file (`edo31`).

/// A scale/octave tuning message, from the byte after its `F0`: the universal
/// header, the device (any is taken), `08`, `08` or `09` for one byte or two a
/// pitch class, three bytes of channel mask, and twelve values. `None` for
/// anything else, a message that stops short, and one whose data bytes are not
/// 7-bit.
///
/// One byte a pitch class is `00` = -64 cents, `40` = 0, `7F` = +63, whole
/// cents. Two bytes is 14 bits, `40 00` = 0, a step of 100/8192 of a cent.

/// A key-tuning message, from the byte after its `F0`.
///
/// - `08 02 tt ll {kk xx yy zz} * ll`: the real-time single-note change, on bank 0.
/// - `08 07 bb tt ll {kk xx yy zz} * ll`: the same on a bank.
/// - `08 01 tt name(16) {xx yy zz} * 128 cs` and `08 04 bb tt ...`: the bulk dumps, which
///   set every key. The checksum is not checked: files write it wrong, and a
///   message that is the right size is the bet every other reader makes.
///
/// `None` for anything else, a message that stops short, and one whose data bytes
/// are not 7-bit. **BASSMIDI reads none of these**; `Config::mts_notes` is what makes
/// them count.
