//! wgpu compute backend.
//!
//! One command buffer per audio block, holding up to five groups of passes:
//! steal, spawn, render, reduce, compact. The live voice count lives in a
//! device-side state buffer rather than in a uniform, which is what lets the
//! whole block be recorded without the host rewriting a uniform between
//! dispatches.
//!
//! Nothing here uses a floating-point atomic. The mixdown is a fixed-order
//! tree reduction, voice slots are assigned from an index rather than an
//! atomic counter, and the only atomics in the codebase are the integer
//! histogram bins in the voice-stealing selection. That is what makes two
//! renders of the same file byte-identical.

/// Words actually allocated per slot for this configuration.
///
/// The last two fields are optional and are allocated from the end. The age is
/// the 25th and is read by the LFOs and by the modulation envelope; the release
/// age is the 26th and only the modulation envelope reads it. A bank that
/// drives neither carries neither. That is not a micro-optimisation: the voice
/// pool is copied in full by every compaction, so one field in twenty-five is
/// 4% of the pool's whole bandwidth, and it measured **5% of a 45-million-note
/// render** when the age was always present.
///
/// The modulation envelope needs the age as well as its own field, so it
/// allocates both even where no LFO asks for the age.
///
