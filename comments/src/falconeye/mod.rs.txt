//! **FalconEye**: Kestrel's error catching and logging, kept apart from the
//! engine it watches. Named by the user, 2026-09-26.
//!
//! Built for 1.2.2, after users lost the GPU mid-render on machines nobody
//! here could reach, and could not say when it stopped, with what settings,
//! or what Windows logged.
//!
//! - [`renderlog`]: a log file for every render, written as it goes.
//! - [`observe`]: what `session` hands the log -- its header, a breadcrumb
//!   about once a second -- and the dev-only failures on purpose.
//! - [`watch`]: a second process that watches each logged render, and
//!   reports a death or a hang the render cannot report itself.
//! - [`winsys`]: the Windows calls that needs. The only `unsafe` here, by the
//!   user's decision; see CLAUDE.md.
//! - [`redact`]: the privacy rule every file FalconEye writes goes through.
//!   The PC's and the account's names are hidden; the MIDI's, the
//!   soundfonts' and the tracks' are not.
//!
//! The engine calls in at two places only: `session::run` opens a log and
//! wraps the front end's observer, and `gpu::device` asks [`winsys`] for
//! DX12's reason when the device is lost.
