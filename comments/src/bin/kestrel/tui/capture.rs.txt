//! A logger that holds records for the screen instead of writing them.
//!
//! The loaders and the render pipeline report through `log`, which on the
//! command line goes straight to stderr. Here it would tear through the
//! progress screen, so records are held and the screen decides what to show:
//! warnings and errors inline and after a render, info lines not at all,
//! because the screen already carries the same facts in its own layout.
