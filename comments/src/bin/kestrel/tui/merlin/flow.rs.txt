//! Merlin's home and the steps of a render in it.
//!
//! The steps are Kestrel's, where Kestrel's do what Merlin needs, and Merlin's
//! where they do not: one MIDI that Merlin will take (31-EDO files and files
//! past its limit are turned back here, before anything is loaded), one
//! soundfont, a read-only look at what the render will ask of the disk in place
//! of the voices, and the typed flags with the ones that mean nothing to
//! Merlin refused. No checkpoint is ever asked for: a merlin render cannot be
//! resumed.
//!
//! The render screen (`progress`) and the end screen (`end`) are Merlin's own.
