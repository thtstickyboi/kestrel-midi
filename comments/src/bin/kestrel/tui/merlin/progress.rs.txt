//! Merlin's render screen: one reader of a `session::Monitor`, as Kestrel's is,
//! drawing what a per-key render has to say.
//!
//! Three stages, which `Snapshot::merlin` says and the monitor puts in the
//! snapshot's phase while the session thread is inside the render's first
//! block. **Arming** is the pre-pass over the whole file, a bar of the bytes
//! read. **Rendering** is the keys, with two bars: the whole render, by the
//! note-ons of the keys done, and the key in hand, by how far through the song
//! its windows have got. **Mastering** is the master handed out through the
//! limiter and the encoder, a bar of its blocks.
//!
//! Ctrl+C stops it at any stage, after a second press, and nothing is kept: a
//! merlin render cannot be resumed, so there is no choice to offer.
