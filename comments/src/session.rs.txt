//! A whole render, and the live view of it that front ends read.
//!
//! [`run`] is the pipeline -- soundfont, MIDI, device, the block loop, the
//! output file -- and every front end goes through it: the command line, the
//! guided renderer, the JSON progress feed, and anything built on this
//! library. What a front end sees of a render in progress is a [`Snapshot`],
//! taken from a [`Monitor`] as often as that front end likes.
//!
//! The render thread only ever copies counters into the monitor, and readers
//! decide for themselves how often to look. A reader polling fast costs the
//! render nothing but the copy, and a reader that stalls cannot stall the
//! render at all.
