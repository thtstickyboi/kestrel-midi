    /// Frames of leading output the driver must run and throw away.
    ///
    /// Zero for a backend that answers `render` with the block it was just
    /// given, which is every per-voice one. A convolution backend cannot: a
    /// note becomes an impulse whose position and gain depend on its
    /// *duration*, so a block cannot be convolved until every note starting in
    /// it has been paired with its note-off, and those arrive later in the
    /// stream. It therefore answers with an earlier block and the driver takes
    /// the offset off the front.
    ///
    /// Not doing so is not a latency: it is the whole render arriving late,
    /// with that much of the opening replaced by silence.
