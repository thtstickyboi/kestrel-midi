//! Merlin's own MIDI front end for the per-key render: the file read straight
//! into paired notes, a group of keys at a time, streaming, with nothing on
//! disk and no driver.
//!
//! Kestrel's driver exists to feed a voice pool: admission, gate tiles,
//! channel rows, a spawn command per layer. Key-major merlin has no pool, and
//! paid for all of it (about 95 ns a note in the bucket pass's `spawn`, about
//! 160 ns a controller event) and for its memory: a dense block was held
//! whole, for every key at once. This reads the file the way the per-key
//! render wants it.
//!
//! 1. **A pre-pass** ([`Prepass::scan`]), every track on its own worker: the
//!    tempo changes and the controllers that move a note's end (sustain,
//!    sostenuto, CC120, CC121, CC123-127) in stream order, the notes on each
//!    key counted per bucket of [`BUCKETS_PER_QN`] to a quarter note, and
//!    where the stream ends. [`Prepass::scan_filed`] also files every note
//!    event by key in a temporary file (`keystore`), so nothing later reads
//!    the MIDI file.
//! 2. **A pass per key group** ([`extract`]): those keys' streams, or the file
//!    read again without a store, a window of ticks at a time sized from the
//!    counts so a window holds about
//!    [`ExtractOpts::window_notes`] of the group's notes. Runs of neighbouring
//!    tracks are read on workers; the group's keys are spread over as many
//!    pairings, each merging its own notes into stream order with a counting
//!    sort and pairing them, side by side.
//!
//! **Pairing is the driver's, exactly** (`GateTable`): a slot's note-offs
//! release its note-ons in order, counted, and the pedals, CC120, CC121 and
//! CC123 act on each slot of their channel from that slot's own counts. So a
//! key's notes and its channels' controllers are all a key's pairing needs,
//! and a group pass holds only its own keys' state. A note's frames are the
//! driver's too: the same [`TempoClock`] walked in stream order, and the end of
//! the file releasing everything at the start of the block its last event
//! falls in.
//!
//! Identical copies -- one slot, one velocity, one start -- are held as one run
//! with a count from the note-on, and released from the front of their slot's
//! queue as note-offs arrive, so a stack of hundreds of copies costs one entry
//! however it is released. What a pass holds is one window of the group's
//! notes and the runs still sounding, not the song.
//!
//! **Checked** by a digest against the driver and the bucket pass, per key
//! and exact, on whole files (`examples/keyparse_bench.rs --gate`) and on
//! made material (`tests/merlin_keyparse.rs`). **Except the sostenuto
//! pedal,** where the driver's counting lets a slot's note-offs overtake its
//! note-ons and so loses a note (the development notes, 2026-10-30); this keeps the
//! queues straight instead, and the two part there.
//!
//! **Not yet a renderer's front end.** It stops at paired notes ([`Note`]):
//! the layers, params and gains the driver builds from them are the next
//! step. 31-EDO files and `--min-velocity` are refused.
