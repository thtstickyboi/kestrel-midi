//! What a per-key merlin render tells whoever watches it, and how it is
//! stopped.
//!
//! The session's own thread cannot say. It sits inside the render's first
//! block until the last key is done, which is minutes to most of an hour, so
//! nothing it polls (`Observer::block`, `Observer::cancelled`) is reached. The
//! render's threads write here instead, and a `Monitor` reads it whenever its
//! reader asks, and sets the stop, which the pre-pass and the render's
//! threads check.
//!
//! Three stages, in order. **Arming** is the pre-pass over the whole file,
//! measured in the bytes of its tracks read. **Rendering** is the keys, each
//! over the whole song; progress is the note-ons of the keys done, with the
//! key in hand counted by how far through the song its windows have got. Keys
//! cost very different amounts (9 to 24 s each on the largest file measured),
//! so the estimate waits for three of them. **Mastering** is the master read
//! out through the limiter and the encoder, a block at a time.
