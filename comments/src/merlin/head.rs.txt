//! The head correction: what the frequency-domain delay line cannot say.
//!
//! `shaders/head.wgsl` carries the derivation. This owns the cache of
//! sustaining waveforms and turns notes into the per-step records the shader
//! walks.
//!
//! The cache is keyed by **waveform**, not by entry: a release bucket changes
//! the envelope and nothing else, so the development notes section 3b's ten buckets per
//! key share one head between them.
//!
//! It also carries more than a correction. A note's body over `[0, Dj)` comes
//! from here in full, because the delay line's kernel starts at `Dj` and is
//! silent before it -- see [`HeadNote::skip`] -- and a note that has not been
//! released yet is emitted from here block by block, before its length is
//! known, by [`sustaining`]. So the row length is **merlin's longest note**,
//! and it is the only length limit left in the backend.

/// waveform and a start read the same frames, and what they add to a bus is
/// `A[n]` times the sum of their envelopes. Black MIDI's chords are made of

/// The live [`HeadNote`]s, filed under every partition step they reach.
///
/// **Why it is not one list.** `head_pass` used to clip every record in one
/// `Vec` against every step. A record lives from the block its note-off is
/// planned in until its end has gone out, which is the emit lag -- 4 blocks, 8
/// steps -- plus its own length, yet it overlaps only the one or two steps it
/// sounds in. Measured on the 44.75 M-note test file: **812.6 million records
/// visited to find 85.8 million that overlapped**, 3.0 s of the pass, 265,000 a
/// step against 28,000 that mattered, and 7.1 M live at the peak step.
///
/// So a record is filed once under each step it reaches ([`HeadNote::steps`])
/// and a step takes its list whole. A copy per step rather than an id into a
/// shared store because a record reaches 1.28 steps on average there (85.8 M
/// filings for the 67.2 M records that reach any step at all) and never many:
/// it is planned once its note-off is known, and everything of it before
/// `emit_start` already went out through the open-note path, so what is left is
/// at most the emit lag and a release. Copies make a step's list one sequential
/// read and retirement free -- the list is dropped when its step is done --
/// where an arena would add a random read per record and a free list to manage.
///
